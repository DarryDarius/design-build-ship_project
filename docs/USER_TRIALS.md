## v1 trial

The participant's name and trial date were unreported. This trial was on a computer.

Ask a graduate student about a recent day that ran late. Then give this task without coaching: “Use the site to plan the rest of your day, choose a version you would actually follow, then imagine you lost another 30 minutes and choose again.” Observe first; ask follow-up questions afterward.

### Participant 1 — desktop, date not supplied

- **Actions reported:** Tried sample day → compared options → confirmed and saved → clicked **Something changed**. Also entered test tasks and protected time, checked an insufficient-time error, and refreshed to verify persistence.
- **What worked:** Could understand the product's purpose and the trade-offs among keeping Project draft, keeping Reading, or doing only must-do work. The calm visual style fit the situation. Protected time stayed protected. The 240-minute must-do task against 165 available minutes produced a specific error with repair options. Editing a draft did not overwrite the confirmed plan; the confirmed plan survived refresh.
- **Friction:** **Something changed** returned to the full form and required the participant to process the disruption manually. The comparison page's large heading delayed the option cards and timeline. Open minutes were stated without showing the open intervals. Completion status required returning to the editor. Reading could not be split. The **Fit everything in** card's KEEP area omitted the must-do Interview preparation task, even though the explanation above named it.
- **Observed hesitation or help requested:** Not reported; do not infer.
- **Could explain choice kept/postponed:** Yes, based on the participant's own description of the three sample options.
- **Could find and revise confirmed plan:** Yes, but described the revision flow as the largest pain point.
- **Exact quote:** “如果只改一个地方，我会优先改 Something changed。用户已经因为意外损失了时间，此时最有吸引力的体验是：‘我又少了 45 分钟’→马上看到更新后的选择。”
- **Other exact quote:** “整体感受：产品定位能理解，方案比较有价值；最需要改善的是‘被打断后，快速调整’的体验。”
- **Issue to fix first:** Give direct lost-time shortcuts and immediate comparison while preserving the confirmed plan until the user confirms a new one.
