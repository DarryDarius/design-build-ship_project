## 60–90 second demo

1. Open the homepage in a fresh browser tab and choose **See a sample plan**. The afternoon starts at 2:00 PM with 210 usable minutes after class.
2. Choose and confirm **Fit everything in**. Then click **Something changed → Start at 2:45 PM (+45 min)**. **In every option** shows the class at 4:00 PM. The revised timeline labels 2:00 PM as the previous start and shows 2:45 PM as the new start. Available time drops to 165 minutes.
3. Compare **Keep Project draft** with **Keep Reading**. The reading option leaves 60 minutes open, in two 30-minute gaps. The draft option leaves one 30-minute gap.
4. Ask which option preserves a whole hour for dinner or rest. Switch to **Leave room to breathe** to show its 90-minute uninterrupted block. Confirm it and show **Last change**.
